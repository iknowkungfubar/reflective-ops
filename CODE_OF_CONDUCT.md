# Code of Conduct

## Standard

Participants are expected to collaborate respectfully and professionally.

Acceptable participation includes:

- discussing ideas rather than attacking people,
- challenging claims with evidence,
- acknowledging uncertainty,
- respecting privacy,
- receiving correction without retaliation,
- making space for disagreement about design and evidence.

Unacceptable participation includes:

- harassment,
- threats,
- discrimination,
- doxxing,
- publication of another person's private information,
- sexual harassment,
- deliberate intimidation,
- repeated bad-faith disruption.

## Scope

This standard applies to project-managed spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other official collaboration channels.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the project.

Reports involving security or sensitive privacy issues should use the private process in `SECURITY.md` rather than a public issue.
