# Disclaimer

ReflectiveOps is an open-source **self-reflection and decision-support toolkit**.

It is not medical care, mental-health treatment, psychotherapy, psychiatric assessment, diagnosis, crisis intervention, legal advice, or a substitute for a qualified professional.

The project may incorporate ideas that also appear in clinical or behavioral research. Their inclusion does **not** mean:

- the skills are clinically validated,
- an AI agent can reproduce a trained professional's judgment,
- an intervention shown to work when delivered by a clinician has been shown to work when delivered by an AI agent,
- generated hypotheses are diagnoses,
- generated role-play reveals another person's actual thoughts or motives.

AI systems can be wrong, overly confident, overly agreeable, incomplete, or contextually inappropriate. Important decisions should be checked against real-world evidence and, where appropriate, trusted humans or qualified professionals.

If someone is in immediate danger or cannot maintain immediate safety, this toolkit is not the appropriate response mechanism; local emergency or crisis resources and direct human support take priority.
