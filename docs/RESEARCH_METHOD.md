# Research Method

ReflectiveOps is research-informed, not clinically validated.

## Evidence preference

For material claims, prefer:

1. high-quality systematic reviews and meta-analyses,
2. authoritative professional or public-health guidance,
3. preregistered or well-designed randomized trials,
4. strong primary studies,
5. established theoretical frameworks,
6. expert commentary.

## Translation rule

Evidence for a psychological mechanism does not automatically validate an AI implementation of that mechanism.

For example:

- evidence for implementation intentions can justify using explicit if-then plans,
- evidence for clinician-delivered psychotherapy does not establish that a chatbot delivers equivalent treatment.

## Claim language

Use language proportional to evidence:

Good:
> “Meta-analytic evidence suggests…”

Avoid:
> “Science proves…”

## Conflicting evidence

If research is mixed:

- say so,
- preserve the uncertainty,
- do not cherry-pick the preferred result.

## Research freshness

When updating research claims, check current literature rather than relying solely on older summaries.
